# Bots and AI assistants

Where to find bots, how to use AI assistants with your own key, what it costs and who sees your conversation with a bot.

## What a bot is in Nevod

A bot is a member of the network just like a person, except that a program answers. Bots can be simple — for example, **Echo** on Nevod Open repeats your messages, which is a handy way to check that the connection works — or **AI assistants** that answer using Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.

## Where to find bots

- **Nodes** → tap a node — its page shows the bots and pages the node offers to its users. Tap a bot to open a chat with it. If you are not registered yet, the app asks for a name or nickname and registers you on that node.
- **Contacts → On this node** — the bots of your home node.

Nevod Open currently runs **AI Assistant**, an all-purpose assistant, and **Tolmach**, a translator.

Bots on private nodes are set up by the node's administrator: which bots you have depends on your node.

## AI assistants with your own key

AI assistants in Nevod have no paid access of their own to Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. A bot answers using **your own key** — so you choose the provider and pay it directly, and neither the node nor the Nevod developers take part in that payment.

**How to connect a key:**

1. Open a chat with an AI assistant. You'll see "This bot answers using your own AI key." → **Add AI key**.
2. If the bot works with several providers, choose the **Provider**: Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI) or Gemini (Google).
3. Get a key on the provider's website — the **Where do I get a key?** link leads straight to the right page. Copy the key and paste it into the **Paste your API key** field.
4. Read where your messages go, turn on **I agree** and tap **Check and save**. The app checks that the key works.

Done — you can write to the bot. To change the provider or key, use the **Change** button in the chat with the bot. One key per provider works for all bots: if a key is already saved, the app offers **Use saved key**.

**All saved keys** — **Profile → Security → AI keys**. **Delete key** is there too.

## What it costs

The provider charges for each request — at its own rates, from the account your key is linked to. For chatting with an assistant these are usually small amounts; some providers have a free tier with limits. Check rates and limits on the provider's website; you can also set a spending limit for the key there — so you never spend more than you decided.

## Who sees your conversation with an AI bot

This is where it differs from ordinary messaging.

- **Your key** is stored only on your phone. With each message it goes to the bot encrypted; the bot uses it for one request to the provider and doesn't keep it.
- **Your messages to the bot** are decrypted by the node the bot runs on and passed to the chosen provider — otherwise the AI couldn't answer. **The provider (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google) sees your conversation with the bot** and processes it under its own rules.
- Between people nothing changes: neither nodes nor AI providers see conversations between people.

So don't send AI bots anything you aren't ready to show the provider: passwords, card numbers, other people's personal data. More in the [Privacy Policy](../../privacy/), section 5.

> AI answers can be inaccurate. Double-check anything important — especially translations of documents and medical, legal and financial questions.
