# Getting started

How to install Nevod, register and send your first message. It takes a couple of minutes, and you need neither a phone number nor an email address.

## What you need

An iPhone or iPad with iOS 17.2 or later. The app is **Nevod** in the App Store. An Android version is in development.

## Cell and node in a nutshell

- A **cell** is you in Nevod: a pair of encryption keys the app creates on your phone. The keys never leave the device, so no one but you and the person you are writing to can read your messages.
- A **node** is a server the app uses to deliver messages. A cell registers on one node — its **home node**. There are public nodes, such as **Nevod Open**, and private ones — for a family, an organization, a community. A node sees whom you write to and when, but not what.

You can write to people on any node of the network, not only your own.

## Registration

1. Open Nevod. The app asks for permission to send notifications — allow it, otherwise you won't know about messages and calls while Nevod is closed.
2. Read the key points of the Terms of Use, turn on **I accept the Terms of Use** and tap **Continue**.
3. **Explore Network** opens — a list of open nodes. You can look around here: nodes, their bots and pages are available without registering.
4. Tap **Start messaging** (top right).
5. Enter **Your display name** — this is how others will see you. Tap **Get started**.

Done: you are registered on the **Nevod Open** node. In a few seconds you will get a welcome message from **administrator**, the node's administration account. There is a blue ✓ badge next to its name.

> The name must be free on this node. If the app says the name is taken, choose another. Names like administrator, admin, support, Nevod can't be taken — so no one can pose as the administration. You can change your name later in your **Profile**.

> **Service accounts are marked.** Every node has its own **administrator** account. On Nevod project nodes, such as Nevod Open, it has a blue ✓ badge — this is the Nevod administration, and the badge is confirmed by the project's signature. On a private node — of a family, an organization, a community — administrator is marked **Node administrator** with the node's name, confirmed by that node's own key. Neither the badge nor the mark can be faked. If a name looks like a service name — administrator, admin, support, Nevod, "Network administrator" and the like — but has neither the ✓ badge nor the mark, an orange ⚠ is shown next to it, and the chat shows the warning "This is not an official Nevod account". Don't give such a person passwords, keys or codes.

## If you were invited to a node

If you received an invitation link or QR code — for example, to your family's or organization's node:

1. Install Nevod and accept the Terms (step 1 above).
2. Open the link or scan the QR code with your phone's camera. On the invitation page tap **Open in Nevod**.
3. The app opens with the node address and code already filled in — enter your name and tap **Get started**.

If "Open in Nevod" didn't work: in the app tap **Start messaging**, enter your name, tap **Have an invite code?** and paste the link into the code field (or scan the QR code with the icon next to the field).

An invite code works once and for a limited time. If the app says "Invalid or expired code — get a new one", ask for a new invitation.

If Nevod on this phone is already registered on another node, the invitation won't move you by itself: one identity has one home node. What to do — see [Contacts](../contacts/), "People on other nodes".

Some nodes admit people by request: the app then shows **Waiting for approval** — the node's administrator will review your request, and you will get a notification.

## What's where in the app

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Chats** | Your conversations. |
| **Calls** | Call history. |
| **Contacts** | The people you talk to; this is also where **Add Contact** is. |
| **Nodes** | Explore Network: nodes, their bots and pages. |
| **Profile** | Your name and avatar, your card for exchanging contacts, invitations, security. |

## Your first message

To write to someone, exchange contacts with them:

1. One of you: **Profile → Share my card** — a QR code appears.
2. The other: **Contacts → Add Contact** — and scans the QR code (or pastes the card text if it was sent to them).
3. Open the contact and write a message.

More in [Contacts](../contacts/).
