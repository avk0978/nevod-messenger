Nevod and other messengers

Most modern messengers encrypt messages. The difference isn't whether there's encryption, but what stays in the company's hands: servers, the user database, phone numbers, the address book, metadata — and what of it can be demanded.

Nevod implements the principle of Zero Knowledge Infrastructure: the developer is cut off from users' data by architecture, not by encryption alone. Information about other messengers reflects their public descriptions as of 2026 and may change.

Why "just encryption" isn't enough

End-to-end encryption protects a message's content in transit. But if a company runs all the servers, keeps an account database keyed by phone numbers and sees who talks to whom and when, it keeps power over the system: it can be compelled, breached or blocked, and an app update can change the rules.

ZKI asks a different question: what could the infrastructure's creator get, even in theory? In Nevod the answer is built into the architecture: keys never leave the device, there's no master key and no backups, no central server and no register of people by phone number.

Low-load distributed network: a comparison

We rate networks against six traits of a low-load distributed network: each node's load is bounded by its own circle of people, not by the size of the whole network. The traits come from Nevod's own principles, so the comparison naturally favors it.

  1. Own circle: a node's load grows with its circle (family, home, team), not with the whole network.
  2. Ordinary hardware: a home computer, mini-server or router; no data center needed.
  3. Economy: text and signals first, heavy data on request.
  4. Delay tolerance: with no connection, the message waits and goes later, including over a slow channel.
  5. No center: no single point of failure, blocking or seizure.
  6. Blind node: a node carries only ciphertext and cannot read it.

1: yes, ½: partly, 0: no. Year: first public release. Class: LL-0, a node's load is bounded by its circle; LL-1, plus bounded transit for others; LL-3, load grows with the whole network or depends on outside users. More: the article.

Signal and WhatsApp get ½ for blindness: content is end-to-end encrypted, but the company sees metadata and runs the servers. In Reticulum and NNCP, intermediate nodes carry and store other people's packets. Nevod still sends media whole, keeps undelivered messages for 72 hours and works only over the internet.

By the main trait, Nevod belongs to class LL-0 together with Briar and Secure Scuttlebutt; Reticulum and NNCP, close to them on the other traits, belong to LL-1. Nevod's difference lies elsewhere: of these networks it is the only one designed as a full messenger for ordinary people, with media, calls and an iPhone app. But it is young: no real users yet, no independent audit, iOS only. The path to six out of six: media on request and delivery without the internet over slow channels.

What we acknowledge

  • A node technically processes only delivery data — which encrypted packet goes to whom — and never sees the content. Nothing stops you from running your own node: then even that delivery data stays with you.
  • Signal's protocol is open and thoroughly reviewed — a strength we acknowledge. Nevod's code is closed for now; opening it is planned.
