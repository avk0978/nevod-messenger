Calls

How to call, answer, turn on video, and what to do if a call doesn't connect.

Calls in Nevod are encrypted from phone to phone. Sound and video go directly between the phones, and if a direct connection isn't possible, through the node's relay, which can't decrypt them either.

Make a call

  • in a chat — the handset icon at the top right;
  • in the Contacts tab — the handset icon in the contact's row;
  • in the Calls tab — tap a call in the history to call back.

You can call any contact, including someone on another node.

When Nevod asks for microphone access for the first time (and camera access for video), allow it — a call is impossible without it.

Answer

An incoming call appears on the regular iPhone call screen — just like a phone call, even if the phone is locked and Nevod is closed. If the app is open, the Nevod screen appears with Accept and Decline buttons.

During a call

ButtonWhat it does
Off / On (microphone)turn your microphone off and back on
Speakerspeakerphone
Bluetoothchoose where the sound goes: the phone, headphones or a Bluetooth headset. Highlighted in blue when the sound goes through Bluetooth
Videoturn on the camera — the call becomes a video call
Camerain a video call — switch between the front and back camera
Endhang up

The top of the screen shows how the connection is going — Calling…, Connecting… — and, once the conversation has started, the call quality: Good, Fair or Poor.

A video call starts as a regular one: call, and when the other person answers, tap Video.

During a call, file transfer is paused so the call isn't disturbed, and it resumes after the call.

If a call doesn't connect

What you seeWhat to do
Busythe other person is already on a call — call later
Connecting… stays on for a long timeone of you has a poor connection. Try switching from mobile data to Wi-Fi or the other way round and call again
the call connected, but one of you can't be heardcheck that Nevod is allowed to use the microphone: iPhone Settings → Nevod → Microphone
incoming calls don't arrive while Nevod is closedcheck that Do Not Disturb or another Focus mode isn't on. If you are on a private node, its administrator should check that the node is connected to the network — without that, a call can't "wake up" the phone

Call history

The Calls tab lists all the calls you made and received. Tap a row to call back. The history is stored only on your phone.
