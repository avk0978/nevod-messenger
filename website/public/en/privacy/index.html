Privacy Policy

Version of 17 October 2026. Nevod is developed by Kolpakov Andrey. Questions about data: privacy@nevod.network.

1. The essentials

Nevod is built on the principle of Zero Knowledge Infrastructure. The content of your messages, photos, videos, voice messages, files and calls is end-to-end encrypted, and keys never leave your device. Neither the network nodes nor the developer can read the content. The one exception is what you write to AI bots yourself, see section 5.

2. What's stored on your device

Your cryptographic identity (keys in the iOS secure storage), contacts, message history and media, settings, and a technical log for diagnostics. The log never leaves the device unless you send it yourself.

This data is not included in iCloud or computer backups and is not transferred to other devices. If the device is lost or reset, or the app is deleted, the history cannot be restored: neither the nodes nor the developer have a copy.

3. What a node processes

  • Your identity's identifier (cell ID) and your public keys.
  • The display name you chose.
  • Device tokens for push notifications and calls.
  • Encrypted messages until they're delivered: undelivered messages are deleted after 72 hours.
  • Your notification settings and your list of blocked users.
  • Delivery metadata (who sends packets to whom and when) and technical logs for diagnostics, kept for a limited time.
  • Your messages to the administrator account (complaints and questions sent from the app). The node's operator reads them in order to reply: they are addressed to the operator. Your conversations with other people remain inaccessible to the operator.

Nodes may belong to different independent operators. The operator of the node you connect to processes this data to run the network.

4. Notifications

Notifications on iPhone are delivered through the Apple Push Notification service. A notification carries the sender's name and the generic text "New message" — never the message content.

5. AI bots

A node may offer AI bots, for example an assistant or a translator. Such a bot answers through the AI provider you pick — Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT) or Google (Gemini) — on your own API key. Nevod doesn't sell AI access and has no keys of its own. You don't need bots to use Nevod.

  • Your key is kept only on your device, in the iOS secure storage, and doesn't move to other devices. It travels to the bot end-to-end encrypted, together with each message. The bot uses it for that one request and doesn't save it.
  • Your messages to a bot. A bot is a separate network participant run by the operator of the node where it's published. On the way, messages to it are end-to-end encrypted like all others; the bot decrypts them to answer and passes the text to the provider you picked. Only write to a bot what you're willing to share with that provider.
  • What the bot keeps: only the last few messages of your conversation, in memory, so it can answer in context; they vanish when the bot restarts. The bot's log records that a request happened, its provider and its length — never the text or the key.
  • The provider receives your messages, the recent conversation and your key, and processes them under its own terms and privacy policy (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google). The requests come from the bot's server, not from your device.
  • Consent. Before you add a key, the app asks you to agree that your messages to bots go to that provider. To withdraw: Profile → Security → AI keys → delete the key. "Reset identity" deletes all keys too.
  • Bots only receive text: photos, files, voice messages and calls are never passed to them.

6. Third parties

We don't sell data or use it for advertising or tracking. We use: Apple (notifications, app distribution), node hosting, Cloudflare (DNS and domain e-mail). If you add an AI key: the provider you picked, for your messages to bots (section 5).

7. Deletion

Profile → "Reset identity" deletes your data on the device and your registration on the node.

8. Children

Nevod is not intended for children younger than the age at which your country allows consenting to data processing on one's own.

9. Changes

We'll announce significant changes to this policy on this page and in the app.
